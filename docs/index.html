Did Oklahoma lose ground in the SEC?

Oklahoma left the Big 12 for the SEC in July 2024. Two seasons in, it wins fewer conference titles (1.5 a year against 2.4), while Texas, which moved the same day, has held steady. But two seasons is too short to rule out luck, and on the national stage OU is as strong as ever.

The short answer

  • Fewer conference trophies, mostly by margin. OU won 3 comparable SEC titles in 2 years. At its recent Big 12 pace it would have expected about 4.8. Several of its "misses" were close: gymnastics lost the SEC meet by 0.275 and then 0.025 points while winning the national title both years.
  • Not yet distinguishable from bad luck. A shortfall this size would happen about 29% of the time even if nothing had changed.
  • Still elite nationally. OU won 3 national championships in its first 2 SEC years (gymnastics (2024-25), gymnastics (2025-26), baseball (2025-26)) and finished #9 and #16 in the Directors' Cup, which scores every sport's national finish.

The Texas test

The best way to separate "the SEC is harder" from "something changed at OU" is to look at the school that made the same move on the same day.

Is OU's gap behind Texas real? Both schools started from the same place, 2.4 titles a year in their last five Big 12 seasons. Since then OU has won 3 and Texas 5. If the two programs were equally strong in the SEC, a split at least this lopsided would happen about 36% of the time by chance. That is too often to call it a real difference yet.

The national picture

The Learfield Directors' Cup awards points for every sport's national finish, so it rewards breadth. Rank 1 is the best athletic department in the country.

How sure are we?

In one sentence

OU won one or two fewer titles than usual, which happens often enough by chance that two seasons cannot prove the SEC move hurt.

The method

We treat each year's titles like a random count with OU's Big 12 average as the expected value (a Poisson model), and ask how often a count this low would show up anyway. For the Texas comparison we ask how often two equally matched programs would split their titles this unevenly.

What could mislead

Titles cluster: softball's regular-season and tournament titles tend to come together, which makes streaks look more meaningful than they are. And the SEC is deeper, so the true title rate should fall. Neither model captures that, which is why we report them as sanity checks.

About the data

  • Big 12 history comes from Wikipedia's champions tables (scraped), with OU's final season (2023-24) added by hand from official sources.
  • SEC seasons are curated by hand: 30 sport-seasons for OU and Texas, each with a source and a confidence level (19 high, 10 medium, 1 low).
